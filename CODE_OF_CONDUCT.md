# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces must be respectful, accessible, and focused on constructive technical collaboration. Welcome different backgrounds and communication styles; give actionable feedback; respect privacy; and assume good intent while addressing concrete impact.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, disclosure of private security reports, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project-affiliated communication. Repository maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this standard.

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers should disclose conflicts of interest and recuse themselves when they are directly involved.
